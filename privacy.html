Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

At Subly Digital Hub, your privacy, account confidentiality, and peace of mind are our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy outlines the explicit data we process when you order digital subscriptions, pre-activated accounts, or communicate with our team.

1. Overview & Data Philosophy

Subly provides digital service brokering, shared profile provisioning, family plan invitations, and social account handovers. We collect only the minimum required information necessary to fulfill your order and verify active warranty coverage. We will never sell, lease, or distribute your contact details to third-party advertisers.

2. Information We Collect

  • Customer Name & WhatsApp Phone Number: Collected during checkout to dispatch credentials, provide activation instructions, and deliver swift customer support.
  • Zero Device or Phone Tracking: We do not ask for, track, or store your phone model, operating system, or hardware identifiers. Only your WhatsApp line is used for fulfillment.
  • Order History & Identifier: Internal reference codes (e.g. SBL-82914) stored in your browser's local storage to enable the Track Order history feature.
  • No Credit/Debit Card Storage: We do not ask for, capture, or store your debit card numbers, CVVs, or bank PINs on our servers. All transactions are handled securely via official peer bank transfers or verified merchant channels.

3. How We Protect Your Credentials

Digital subscriptions (such as Netflix profiles, Spotify invites, ChatGPT Plus credentials, and VPN accounts) are transmitted exclusively via encrypted 1-on-1 WhatsApp communications directly between you and our verified operator. We advise all customers to follow our security guidelines upon receiving private accounts (such as updating recovery emails or passwords where permitted).

4. 100% Active Warranty & Replacement Terms

All subscriptions purchased through Subly come with our active warranty promise:

  • If your assigned profile or account experiences any disruption, screen lock, or credential reset during your paid subscription tenure, our team will investigate and restore or replace the account within our 5-minute response window.
  • Warranties are voided only if a customer violates individual provider rules (such as altering billing info or reselling single-user slots to unauthorized parties).

5. Browser Local Storage Usage

Our web application uses client-side LocalStorage exclusively to store:

  • Your theme preference (Dark Mode / Light Mode).
  • Your active customer session for fast, 1-click checkout auto-filling.
  • Your recent purchase history so you can track order statuses without repetitive logins.

You can wipe this data at any time by clearing your browser cache or clicking "Clear History" on the Orders Page.

Response Time: Typically under 5 minutes during live hours (24/7 coverage).
